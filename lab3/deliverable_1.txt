MIT VNAV Lab 3 — Deliverable 1
Quaternion transformations in ROS 1 / Noetic

A. Message vs. tf2

1. Convert an incoming geometry_msgs quaternion into an existing tf2 quaternion:
   tf2::fromMsg(quat_msg, quat_tf);

2. Convert a tf2 quaternion into a newly declared geometry_msgs quaternion:
   geometry_msgs::Quaternion quat_msg = tf2::toMsg(quat_tf);

3. Read the scalar (w) component of a tf2::Quaternion:
   quat_tf.getW();

B. Conversion

1. Extract yaw from tf2::Quaternion quat_t in one function call:
   const double yaw = tf2::getYaw(quat_t);

2. Convert geometry_msgs::Quaternion to an Eigen 3-by-3 rotation matrix:
   Eigen::Quaterniond q;
   tf2::fromMsg(quat_msg, q);
   const Eigen::Matrix3d R = q.toRotationMatrix();

   The first call converts the ROS message to an Eigen quaternion; the second
   obtains its rotation matrix. In a ROS 1 Noetic source file, include the
   tf2_geometry_msgs and tf2_eigen conversion headers as needed.
